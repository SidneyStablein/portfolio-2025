Social Media Content & Digital Campaigns

Goal:
Produce social media graphics and ads for various platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn to increase visibility and engagement.
Approach:
I created platform-specific designs that were both engaging and informative. From static posts to animated ads, I tailored each piece to suit the tone the content, while ensuring they adhered to Service Express’s brand guidelines. During my internship I made static & animated social media templates on Adobe Illustrator and Canva for consistency and to help non-designers create content. I spent time making them as user-friendly as possible, since I knew this would be some individuals first time interacting with design software.
Outcome:
The templates were a hit! Many non-designers were able to use the templates and are still using them to post content. The main issue we saw with non-designer employees posting content was that it wasn't adhering to the company's creative guidelines. Now posts are more uniform and consistent across employees accounts and Service Express’s pages.
