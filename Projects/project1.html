Redesigns for Customer Case Studies

  • Goal: Redesign and modernize the layout of customer case studies to improve readability, engagement, and align with the company’s evolving brand identity.
  • Approach: I collaborated with cross-functional teams in both the US and UK to ensure consistency in design. I focused on creating visually appealing layouts that highlighted key information and case study success stories while implementing the new brand elements.
  • Outcome: The redesigned case studies contributed to improved user engagement, providing a cleaner, more professional look that helped Service Express’s clients better showcase their success with the company and provided our sales representatives with more content.
